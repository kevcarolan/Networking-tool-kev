Good to know

What counts, and what happens at renewal

What is a device

Anything the server logs in to or monitors: a switch, router, firewall, controller, UPS card, PDU or room probe. Racks, servers and patch panels in the inventory are free.

Cisco Meraki

Each Meraki organisation you connect counts as 25 devices, however many it holds.

If a licence runs out

Backups, checks and monitoring keep running, and any upgrade already under way finishes. Only adding devices and starting new upgrade jobs wait for the new licence.

Changing your plan

More devices, a bigger edition or the add-on: you pay the difference for the rest of the year and get a new licence file with the same end date.
