What we collect, and why

  • When you email us, for example to ask for a trial: your name, email address, organisation and what you tell us about your network. We use it to answer you, send the download and follow up on the trial. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or taking steps towards a contract you've asked about.
  • When you buy: the name, email and billing address of the buyer, and the licence we issue. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see card numbers. The legal basis is performing the contract, and keeping accounting records as the law requires.
  • The web host's logs: like every website, the server that hosts this one records the address and browser of each visit for a short time, for security.

The product itself

The product runs on your server. It sends nothing to us: no usage data, no device data and no licence checks over the internet.

Who else sees it

  • Stripe, for payments and invoices.
  • Our email and file-sharing providers, which hold our messages and the download links we send.

We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Enquiries for two years after our last contact. Customer and licence records for as long as you're a customer and then as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
